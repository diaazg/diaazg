## 🔬 Research

**AraMS-28k / RefLAM — Weakly Supervised Annotation & Restoration of Historical Arabic Manuscripts** *(Graduation Thesis)*

- **RefLAM**: a reference-grounded annotation pipeline pairing a multimodal-LLM OCR labeller with a fuzzy sequence-alignment engine against clean ground-truth editions, accelerating transcription while preserving full human review.
- **AraMS-28k**: a 28,600-line, 14-book, four-script (Naskh, Maghrebi, Ruq'ah, lithographic) dataset with line- and margin-level annotation.
- **Recognition-aware restoration**: a U-Net trained with a frozen transformer critic in the loss, improving OCR accuracy on genuinely damaged manuscript lines where a standard pixel-loss restorer showed no gain.
- **Cross-script benchmark**: quantified a previously undocumented Eastern–Maghrebi generalization gap (6.48% → 37.88% CER).

**TEAVAD — Multimodal Framework for Video Anomaly Detection**

- Reimplemented and extended a published framework fusing visual and textual features, using Vision Transformers (ViViT, MViT, MViTv2) for richer spatio-temporal representations.
- Explored SimCSE and later DiffCSE to strengthen the textual branch through contrastive learning.
